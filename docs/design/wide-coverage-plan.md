## 2. Coverage metric

Unit: a corpus row in `data/greek-clitics/corpus.jsonl` (Greek) or `data/english/corpus.jsonl` (English native, TTR, robot) with `judgment.status` in `{licensed, starred, variable, not_stated}`; `not_stated` rows are counted separately and never enter a rate. `scripts/corpus_run.py --grammar G --corpus F --top-n N` calls `dylan.workbench_api.parse_request` per row (the oracle pattern of `tests/test_greek_workbench.py:25-38`) and records `ok`, `complete`, `failure.kind`, `judgment.status`, semantics, `stats` (M0 E0.1), `top_n_cuts`, `cap_hit`, ms, and `executable` (every token has a `Lexicon.lookup` hit or is a parser control, the test at `greek_workbench.py:259`). `scripts/coverage_report.py` computes four rates per (phenomenon tag, grammar), each as `k/n` with `n` shown:

1. Vocabulary: executable rows / rows.
2. Construction agreement: executable rows whose outcome matches the judgment (licensed: `ok and complete`; starred: not complete; variable: at least two complete readings with different strategy tags, from E4.5).
3. Semantic correctness: agreeing licensed rows whose `expected.semantics_substrings` match and, for mltt grammars, whose `to_coq()` compiles with `coqc`.
4. Overgeneration: starred rows that complete, listed by id, reported as a red number, never folded into agreement (must be 0 at every acceptance check).

Undergeneration (licensed executable rows that do not complete) is listed by id with the cascade's `failure.kind` and, when present, the idea 9 `model_estimate` in a separate column. Matrix letters: C when agreement is at least 0.9 with n at least 3, P when any agreeing row exists, A otherwise. Search-cost columns (median tuples, `attempt_backtrack` calls, ms) sit beside the rates so a milestone that raises coverage while multiplying search is visible. The committed `data/greek-clitics/coverage.lock` (and `data/english/coverage.lock`) stores per-tag counts; `tests/test_clitic_corpus.py::test_coverage_lock_does_not_regress` fails when any tag's agreeing count drops or overgeneration is non-zero; `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs `corpus_run.py --all && coverage_report.py --check` after `uv run pytest -q`. Jev columns (`model_labels`, `model_estimate`) never enter any rate; a fifth optional column reports model-human agreement per tag with `n`.

Phenomenon tags, fixed in M0 and reused by idea 9 buckets: `placement negation na imperative gerund prohibitive cluster pcc doubling clld htld climbing auxiliary dp_case svo vso ovs wh relative coordination ethical_dative diachronic` for Greek; `name indefinite universal_subj universal_obj definite adjective adverb complement relative_subj relative_obj wh_subj wh_obj yes_no coordination_prop coordination_vp negation tense_aux pronoun pp_adjunct pp_arg ditransitive copula_adj copula_np imperative` for English. Dialect ids stay `smg cypriot pontic` plus history ids `koine mainland cyprus pontus`; `grico`, `salentino`, `cretan` go in `dialect_extended`; `wh`, `fronted_constituent`, `gerund` in `environment_extended`.

## 4. How Jev widens coverage

Jev never adds a sentence, a rule, a template, a lexicon row to a shipped file, or a judgment; the engine and the sourced judgments do. It widens coverage indirectly, each mechanism tied to an idea number and a gate:

1. Corpus throughput (idea 3; M5, M6): pre-filled `dialect`, `environment`, `placement`, `has_cluster`, `pcc_relevant`, `clitic_climbing`, `clitic_doubling`, `diachronic` suggestions on 150 to 400 transcribed rows, reviewed instead of typed. Gate: 18/20 `environment` and 20/20 `has_cluster` on the CASES rows; `placement` only if idea 5 condition A passed. The star never comes from Jev.
2. Backlog ordering (idea 9; M3 onward): every uncovered row gets a `construction_family` and `distance_from_coverage` estimate in a separate column, so grammar sessions open on the family with the most rows one lexicon row or one rule away. Gate: 0.80 family agreement on 30 rows before the column is shown; `t_route = 0.60`; a wrong route costs one moved row.
3. Entry prior (idea 1; M2 onward): once `clitic(REF,PERSON,CASE)` and plain/neg/na verb rows give real multi-entry Greek words, the prior orders DAG children so the first traversed child is the winning one, keeping the "traversal constant while tuples grow linearly" regime as k grows. Gate: 15/16 Greek entries at `p(top) > 0.7` in M0 step 0, then 0.85 top-1 on 40 ambiguous Greek words in replay; tie-break only, `t_prior = 0.50`.
4. Fan-out ordering (idea 2; M4 onward): once `*ADJUNCTION`, `LATE *ADJUNCTION`, TOPIC, LINK and `merge` multiply prefixes, ranked siblings cut dead-end traversals and keep wide grammars under the 30 s worker deadline. Gate: 10 points over the comparator on 30 Greek fan-outs, fewer successful backtracks, suite byte-identical under stub and recorded answers; `t_order = 0.50`; ordering only.
5. Later, English only (idea 6 with idea 4 as the typed second column; M7 hand-off): Choice-only lexicon candidates into a review queue. Gate: idea 8 harness bars, then exact recovery 0.85 on the 34 bundled forms and lemma accuracy 0.95 on the 69 `s3` rows, then decision D8.

Off limits, unchanged from the ideas doc section 3: Jev as grammaticality judge (no output sets `judgment.status`, `failure.kind`, `ok`, `complete`, a test assertion; `constraint_violation` is never a triage option; idea 5's `licensed` Noul does not run without decision D9); Jev as licenser, pruner or verifier (which children exist, which merge candidates exist, which proposals reach the validator is engine business; no beam in v1, and no Jev tie-break inside any future beam cut); Jev generating text (lemma, symbol, PRED names, LOFT triggers, action programs, formulas, Coq, LaTeX, Greek transliteration); Jev live in the test suite (stub or recorded answers only, since probabilities drift 0 to 2.2 percent between passes); counting and arithmetic (clitic count, open requirements, incompleteness are computed in code and sent as fields); referent resolution itself (Jev may order `Substitute.candidates` once E2.4 enumerates them, a separate decision D10); answer leakage (`CASES[i]["explanation"]`, `DIALECTS[d]["explanation"]`, gloss text stating the rule); writing to shipped grammar files, `LEXICA`, `GATES`, `lexicon.txt`, `PROTECTED`, `CASES`; semantic judgments on readings or scope. Greek calibration always comes first: no Greek-state hook goes live without the M0 numbers, every Greek result is reported for Greek script and ASCII separately, and the vendor states no Greek figure.

## 7. Risks

- No `TYPESAFE_API_KEY`: every Jev item stays in stub, gates are unmeasured; coverage is unaffected but "How Jev widens coverage" delivers nothing. The plan states this per milestone rather than letting it slip silently.
- Greek accuracy: the vendor states no figure and independent non-English results drop 3 to 11 points; a failed M0 gate confines Jev to English states, which is the intended outcome of the gate, not a failure of the plan.
- The `put` clash covers only differing restrictions; the M1 guard `¬Ex.Fo(x)` and the E-probe on `το τον ξέρω.` are what prevents overgeneration on the very string promoted out of `construction_gap`. Overgeneration is a hard zero at every acceptance check.
- `subsumes` direction: the bug at `node.py:123` is the same check E2.1 and E2.3 need; both carry the explicit `0P` against `010`, `0110`, `00` test so the direction cannot flip twice.
- Unfixed nodes before MERGE: `star-adjunction` in a grammar without the elimination guard adds a dead-end child to every sentence and lets incomplete trees complete; hence the M4 bundling.
- `?</\0>Ty(...)` case filters were reasoned from `Requirement` handling, not executed; E3.1 probes before any DP template.
- `?(Fo(a) || <\/+>Fo(a))` as a requirement over a disjunction is unverified in the factory; E5.1 probes and has a two-label fallback.
- Fan-out in a real thesis grammar is unknown; the k x m fixture is a proxy. `ParseStats`, `top_n_cuts` and `cap_hit` in every corpus run show the regime as it appears, and E4.3, E4.4, E4.6 stay opt-in until measured.
- Transcription volume (150 to 400 rows from a PDF with math Greek transliterations) is the largest agent cost and the source of most labelling errors; every row carries page or line, and `human_labels` records the review pass.
- Thesis per-chapter example counts vary by counting method; targets are stated as minimums (150 rows, 40 starred) rather than as verified totals.
- Effort: about 24 orchestrator sessions and 12 agent days to M7; the English track adds coverage from M1 without extra engine work, but English lexicon growth (thousands of rows) is deliberately outside this schedule.
- Three byte-identical `2015-english-ttr` copies and the dead 2015 `merge`/`*late-star-merge` rules are touched only through `sync_grammars.py` and E2.3's side effect; re-enabling them is not scheduled and the fragile tests named in `docs/design/maps/tests-ci.md` are not at risk.
