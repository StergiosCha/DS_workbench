SMG Greek spellings and delta variants are aliases of the source transcriptions. The plural defaults `we` and `you_group` have person and number restrictions but do not yet distinguish collective and distributive readings. Past verb forms do not yet contribute tense quantification.

The Pontic first/second-person cluster is a reported weak-PCC pattern for some speakers; the thesis explicitly cautions against generalizing it to every Pontic variety. The `m` entry builds a fixed indirect-object node and an unfixed direct-object node. Its required continuation prevents completion after bare `m` or with a different following word. The independent use of reduced `a` and the full-DP imperative example await the DP milestone.
